Your ebooks, scrolling at your pace.

Drag them anywhere on the page to add them. DRM-protected and password-locked books, including Kindle store purchases, can't be opened.

    Reading guide
    Magnifier

    Turn it on with the magnifier button or the M key. Drag its rim to move it, or the dot on the rim to resize it.

    Motion

    Moves the text in fractions of a pixel so it glides instead of stepping. Turn it off if text looks slightly soft while it scrolls.

    Auto measures your screen. Choose a rate yourself if motion still judders, for example on a monitor that changes its rate.

    PDF pages

    Text size, typeface and spacing apply when the PDF is shown as reflowed text.

    Shortcuts
    Space play / pause · tap the text to do the same
    ↑ ↓ faster / slower
    [ ] smaller / larger text
    H switch between pointer and hand tool
    M magnifier on / off
    F full screen on / off
    Esc close panel, then back to library

    Book details

    Cover

    Drag the large preview to choose which part of the image shows.

    Images are shaped to a 2:3 book cover automatically. You can also paste an image, or drop one onto the large preview.

    Public databases

      Appearance

      Colours for the library and panels. The reading page has its own colours under Display in the reader.

      Theme
      Accent colour
      Background

      Pick a light colour and it's used in the light theme; pick a dark one and it's used in the dark theme. Velvet Scroll makes a matching shade for the other theme, and adjusts the text and accent so they stay easy to read.

      Backup and restore

      Save a backup

      One file with your books, covers, reading positions, shelves and settings. Keep it on a USB drive or in your own cloud storage, then restore it in Velvet Scroll on another computer or browser.

      With a password, the backup is encrypted with AES-256 and can't be opened without it. A forgotten password can't be recovered.

      Restore a backup

      Adds the books from a backup to this library. For books you already have, the most recent reading position and details are kept.

      Browser sync

      Uses your browser's own sync between computers where you're signed in to the browser with extension syncing turned on. The books themselves aren't synced: copy them once with a backup, and after that your place follows you.
